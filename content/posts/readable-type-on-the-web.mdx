Most web type problems are not font problems. They are rhythm problems.

**Line length first.** Between 45 and 75 characters per line is the classic window, and it holds. A screen that is full of text at 120 characters per line is a wall; the same text at sixty is a page.

## The fast check

- Tune `max-width` before you tune size.
- Keep line-height around 1.5–1.7 for body copy; tighten it as the type gets bigger.
- Paragraph margins belong to the rhythm, not the typography — pick a consistent unit and stick to it.

## Dark mode is a size problem

On dark surfaces, thin strokes dissolve. The fix is nearly always weight, not color: bump the font-weight of body text by one step and the page gets quieter, then louder in the right places.
